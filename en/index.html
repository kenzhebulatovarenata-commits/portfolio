ABOUT ME

Hi! My name is Renata Kenzhebulatova. I am studying for a BA in Graphic Design at the British Higher School of Art and Design. I have also completed a year-long Motion Design and CG course at Bang Bang Education.

Every year, I develop my skills across illustration, typography, layout, identity and animation. Working on a wide range of projects helps me keep exploring and growing creatively.

I enjoy working both independently and as part of a team. I like exploring the technical and creative sides of different disciplines in depth and continually improving my skills.

Contact me

TOOLS

I use Cinema 4D and After Effects for animation; Photoshop, Illustrator and Affinity Designer for illustration and graphics; Blender for 3D scenes and objects; and InDesign for layout.

EXPERIENCE

3D modelling and animation

I work with 2D and 3D graphics, creating animation, illustration and visual effects in a range of formats. I develop projects from the initial idea to the final outcome, including concept, design and production. I am confident with several professional tools and choose them to suit each project, from static graphics to animation and 3D.

Identity and visual systems

I develop identities from concept to completion, from key visuals and headlines to merchandise and communication. I create cohesive visual systems and adapt them to different formats, including animation, banners and digital communication.

Layout and typography

I design layouts for print and digital formats, working with text, composition, typography and graphics. I develop books, booklets and zines from the initial idea to final production.
