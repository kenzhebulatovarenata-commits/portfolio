DEGREE SHOW 2026

This team project focused on developing an identity for Universal University’s Degree Show 2026. We created a visual system that reflects the event’s concept and brings its different disciplines into a unified communication. Our main focus was a flexible visual language that could adapt to different applications and formats.

Across all applications, we aimed to preserve a visual principle that could adapt to different formats. On posters, animations and other materials, the shapes change in scale and colour to make the system varied and flexible. This gives the identity a dynamic quality and lets it respond to different compositions. Patterns, typography and the isometric grid keep it recognisable, cohesive and informative throughout.

Final animation

Animations

In the animation, shapes move along the grid, suggesting motion, freedom and the possibility of growing in any direction. Although the compositions change, every element remains part of a shared structure.

The result is a system that connects the different disciplines within Degree Show 2026 while allowing the student work to remain the focus. We wanted the graphic language to become part of the exhibition, helping projects, spaces and audiences communicate with one another.
