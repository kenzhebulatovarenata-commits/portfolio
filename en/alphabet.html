ALPHABET

This zine is an alphabet in which each letter stands for one of my favourite things. Each is explored through three forms of inquiry: philosophical, physical and historical, revealing different aspects of its meaning and origins. The objects are organised into five thematic groups, each reflecting a part of my personality.
