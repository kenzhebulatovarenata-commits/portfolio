Pragma x BHSAD

Limited-edition collection

Developed in collaboration with Yandex Pragma, this project proposes a festive collection for Maslenitsa. It includes illustrations, tableware, packaging and decorative objects. The idea centres on Maslenitsa as a celebration that brings people together through conversation and time spent with one another.

The circle dance became a visual metaphor for this idea. Moving together, individuals form a single composition while retaining their own identities. This principle shaped the entire collection, from illustrations of universal characters to patterns, colour combinations and the decoration of objects.

Particular attention went into adapting the graphics to different formats. Circular compositions follow the shape of the tableware, the pattern works as a standalone decorative element, and the circle dance becomes the main focus of the packaging. This keeps the visual system cohesive while giving each object its own composition.
